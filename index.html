    Early alpha, honestly

    In now: the In-Between, where you wake, and the Hall of Time, a black shore under a night storm. Lightning there leaves moments behind to fight, and a fire at its edge keeps a trader, a stash and a waypoint. One hero, abilities you choose as you earn them, a dodge roll, life and mana flasks, loot with affixes and levels. It plays on a computer, or with touch controls on a phone.

    Progress saves on its own as you play. Close the tab and you pick up where you left off. While the game is in alpha, progress may be reset.

    Tell us what you think: the game asks at the end of the alpha, and Feedback in the pause menu works any time.

    The game is open source under the MIT licence: read the code or report a bug on GitHub. Models, animations and textures come from free libraries, listed in the asset credits.
