    Early alpha, honestly

    In now: a first act with a town, two roads of two zones each, a boss off each road and a last zone behind the town's gate. One hero with two storm skills, a dodge roll and life and mana flasks, loot with affixes, levels and a small skill tree. It takes well under an hour, on a computer or with touch controls on a phone.

    Progress saves on its own as you play. Close the tab and you pick up where you left off.

    The game is open source under the MIT licence: read the code or report a bug on GitHub. Models, animations and textures come from free libraries, listed in the asset credits.
